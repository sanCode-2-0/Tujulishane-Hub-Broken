What is the RMNCAH Coordination Hub?

The RMNCAH Coordination Hub is a national digital platform providing real-time visibility into program activities across Kenya. It maps who is working where and on what, helping reduce fragmentation, avoid duplication, and improve alignment of partner efforts.

Key Features

  • ✔ Interactive dashboard with national RMNCAH visibility
  • ✔ Geospatial mapping of partners and interventions
  • ✔ Real-time updates and collaboration tools
  • ✔ Supports coordinated planning & resource allocation
  • ✔ Enhances transparency and strengthens decision-making

Frequently Asked Questions

The RMNCAH Coordination Hub is an official Ministry of Health digital platform designed to enhance visibility, coordination, and accountability among stakeholders implementing projects in Reproductive, Maternal, Newborn, Child, and Adolescent Health (RMNCAH). It enables partners and donors to register, submit projects, collaborate, and align efforts with national health priorities.

The platform streamlines coordination between the Ministry of Health, implementing partners, and donors. It ensures transparency in project implementation, minimizes duplication of efforts, and provides an up-to-date view of ongoing initiatives through interactive maps and project dashboards.

Visit the registration tab on the access page, choose your organization role (Donor, Partner, MOH), fill in details, upload verification documents, and submit for two-tier admin review. You'll receive email notification once approved.
