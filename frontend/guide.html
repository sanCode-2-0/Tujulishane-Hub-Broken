01

Platform Overview

The RMNCAH Coordination Hub is the Ministry of Health’s central digital repository designed to align, track, and coordinate health initiatives across Kenya’s 47 counties.

Implementing Partners

Register organizational profiles, submit health projects, attach proposals, and invite internal co-owners.

Donors & Supporters

Track funded initiatives across counties, explore thematic distributions, and review project outcomes.

02

Account Registration & Login

  1. Registration: Navigate to Go to Page and select your role (Partner or Donor). Fill out your organization details, contact email, and password.
  2. Email Verification: After submitting, check your email inbox for a verification link to confirm your registered email address.
  3. One-Time Password (OTP) Login: Upon entering valid credentials, a 6-digit OTP code will be dispatched to your registered email. Enter the OTP within 10 minutes to authorize access.
  4. Account Approval: Partner accounts require administrative review by the Ministry of Health before project submission rights are granted.
03

Submitting a New Project

Click "Add New Project" to access the 6-step project entry wizard:

  • Step 1: Location — Pin exact sub-county locations and facilities on the interactive Mapbox map.
  • Step 2: Basic Info — Select Category (Implementing, Research with Lacoste #, or Priority), Title, and Time Period.
  • Step 3: Health Themes — Select applicable health thematic areas (GBV, AYPSRH, MNH, FP, CH, AH, Advocacy & SBC, M&E, Research).
  • Step 4: Contact Person — Provide designated contact person name, role, and email address.
  • Step 5: Budget & Goals — Specify currency (KES/USD/EUR), total budget amount, and key objectives.
  • Step 6: Documents — Upload supporting PDF, DOCX, or XLSX proposals (up to 10MB per file).
04

Two-Tier Review & Approval Process

To ensure quality control and thematic alignment, projects undergo structured review before publishing:

Tier 1 — Thematic Review: Assigned Subject Matter Experts (Thematic Reviewers) evaluate technical methodology, location coverage, and thematic alignment.
Tier 2 — Executive Approval: Ministry of Health Approvers grant final approval to publish the project to the live national database.
05

Managing Project Collaborators

Collaborators can be invited to participate in managing or viewing project progress:

Timing Rule: The collaborator invitation panel appears on the Project Details page after the project has received official approval (APPROVED status).

Roles Available: Co-Owner (full editing & collaborator management rights), Editor (project updates), and Viewer (read-only monitoring).

06

Government Priorities & Call for Proposals

Ministry of Health Administrators issue official calls for proposals and announcements under "Government Priorities". Partners can review guidelines, submit collaboration requests, or ask questions via integrated project messaging.

07

Filtering & Exporting CSV Data

Use the top search bar and filter controls on the Projects Dashboard to narrow projects by County, Sub-County, Health Theme, Date Range, or Status. Click "Export CSV" to download structured datasets for offline reporting.
