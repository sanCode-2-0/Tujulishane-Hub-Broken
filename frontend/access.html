Full FAQ Page
The RMNCAH Coordination Hub is an official Ministry of Health digital platform designed to enhance visibility, coordination, and accountability among stakeholders implementing projects in Reproductive, Maternal, Newborn, Child, and Adolescent Health (RMNCAH).
Approved RMNCAH stakeholders including partner organizations (NGOs, CBOs, private sector implementers), donor organizations, and Ministry of Health (MOH) staff can register. Each account type requires verification and approval by the MOH admin.
Once submitted, your account goes through a two-step validation process by the MOH reviewer and administrator. This typically takes up to 12 hours. You will receive an email confirmation once approved.
